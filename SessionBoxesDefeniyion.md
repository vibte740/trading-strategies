What sits between London and NY (UTC, summer time):

LCH (1000-1200): London lunch. It is usually quiet and choppy, which is the gap between your LONDON and NY AM boxes.
LD/NY (1200-1600): the overlap, the highest liquidity and volatility of the day. Your NY AM and LONDON CLOSE boxes both sit inside it.
GAP (1700-1830): the lull between London close and NY PM.
RLL (2100-0000): the rollover and low-liquidity window before Asia opens.
